## QK-DEC-121 v2 supersession overlay

The tables below preserve their F2.0 mappings. QK-F2E-013 is a superseded v1
B/C experiment and is not an input to the current single-B topology. OD-02
remains open for the exact production card, applet and protocol, but its v2
input must come from a later registration covering the required B card,
optional setup-only byte-equivalent spare, the prohibition on Card C and
post-setup second-card creation, and the Kit-Restore replacement boundary.

This document maps F2 experiments to the owner decisions their future
evidence may inform. It contains placeholders only: no evidence exists, no
input has been produced, and this document makes no recommendation of any
kind.

Measurement authorizes nothing. An experiment outcome, however clear, is
only an input. Every owner decision is resolved exclusively by an explicit
owner entry in `docs/DECISION-LOG.md`; no accumulation of F2 evidence can
resolve, pre-commit, or default any decision.

## Discipline

  produces an evidence record in `docs/f2/EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md`, and even
  then it remains an input, not a conclusion.
- This document never contains a preferred option, a ranking, a candidate
  endorsement, or any other recommendation.
- OD-02 remains STOP-BEFORE-F3: it must be resolved by the owner before any
  F3 card-dependent work, regardless of what F2 evidence accumulates.

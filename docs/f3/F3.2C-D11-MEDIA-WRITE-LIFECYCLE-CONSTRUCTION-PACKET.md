## Mandatory contradiction warning

The D-10 owner record says “Failure releases no artifact or partial output.” QK-REQ-TRN-008 only requires interrupted/incomplete output not be presented as complete. These are not equivalent. D-10 may prohibit any signed-PSBT bytes or transport fragments becoming observable through either route after an attempt later fails, including QR frames already displayed and SD prefixes or objects already written, or it may only prohibit recognizing or presenting an incomplete route representation as a complete signed-PSBT output. Interrupted QR cannot retract observed frames, and a receiver may accumulate frames across interrupted or retried cycles. Conventional SD staging cannot guarantee no residue. Neither route may silently assume the weaker interpretation. D-11 selection is blocked until owner meaning is resolved.

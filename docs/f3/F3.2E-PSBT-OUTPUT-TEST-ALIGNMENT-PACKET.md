## Conflict analysis

### DIFF-002 conflict

The canonical DIFF-002 plan/oracle text refers to "finalized output." Under D-10 (recorded in the F3.2b owner-response record), QuietKey acts only as a BIP174 Signer and the sole releasable artifact is an unfinalized signed PSBT; QuietKey never finalizes, never extracts a raw transaction, and never broadcasts. The phrase "finalized output" is therefore stale and ambiguous under D-10: it would not occur in an output produced under the D-10 direction, and the oracle cannot be satisfied by finalization evidence that D-10 prohibits.

### DIFF-004 conflict

The canonical DIFF-004 plan/oracle text refers to "the signed PSBT and raw final transaction exported via QR and via SD." Under D-10, QuietKey never creates or releases a raw final transaction, never finalizes, and never extracts. The phrase "raw final transaction" in the DIFF-004 plan directly conflicts with D-10; such an artifact cannot appear in DIFF-004 outputs produced under the D-10 direction.
